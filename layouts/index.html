Numerical methods, in pure Go.
Ported line by line, checked step by step.

go-numerics is the numerical layer of the Go scientific stack built on go-ndarray: optimisation and root finding, quadrature and ODE solvers, and special functions. Each library ports its reference algorithms — MINPACK, L-BFGS-B, QUADPACK, Radau, BDF, Cephes, Boost.Math — statement by statement, and its tests replay recorded answers: SciPy runs, the netlib Fortran, and mpmath at high precision. No cgo, no assembly.

Why go-numerics

The numerical calls notebooks make most.

A census of 339 scientific notebooks set optimize's order — minimize, minimize_scalar, scalar roots, root, least_squares and curve_fit, linprog — and integrate and special cover quad, solve_ivp and the functions every statistic is computed with. Each Go function names the reference call it corresponds to.

📉

Optimisation and roots

Nelder–Mead, Powell, CG, BFGS, Newton-CG and L-BFGS-B; SLSQP with bounds, equality and inequality constraints; Brent, Ridder and Newton for scalar roots; MINPACK's hybrid method for systems.

📈

Least squares and fits

Levenberg–Marquardt and trust-region reflective with bounds and robust losses; CurveFit with the parameter covariance; linear programming with duals.

∫

Quadrature

QUADPACK ported from the netlib Fortran: singularities, infinite ranges, oscillatory and algebraic weights, Cauchy principal values; double, triple and N-dimensional integrals.

🌀

ODE solvers

RK45, RK23, DOP853, Radau and BDF with events and dense output, and an automatic Runge–Kutta/BDF switch for problems that turn stiff.

Γ

Special functions

Gamma, beta, erf and the normal distribution, incomplete gamma and beta and their inverses, Bessel functions of real order, zeta, elliptic integrals.

🧮

On go-ndarray

Curve-fit data, covariances, LP matrices and ODE solutions are *ndarray.Array; special/nd applies a function element-wise with broadcasting, across cores.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

No cgo, no assembly. CI runs the tests on amd64 and arm64 and under emulation on riscv64, loong64, ppc64le and s390x.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

Every repository of the organization. Ported code keeps its attribution in place.
